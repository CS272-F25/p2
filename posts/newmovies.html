New Movie Releases This Fall

Downton Abbey: The Finale

A graceful farewell to an era of tradition, family, and change.

Downton Abbey: The Grand Finale picks up in 1930, following the Crawley family and their staff into a period of immense social and cultural transformation. A public scandal erupts surrounding Lady Mary’s divorce from Henry Talbot, creating a social dilemma that threatens both her reputation and Downton’s legacy.

Financial trouble soon follows when an American guest is revealed to be a con artist plotting to steal the family fortune, placing the future of the estate in serious jeopardy. As tensions rise, the household must unite to protect their home and restore stability.

In a long-awaited turning point, Mary and Edith finally put years of rivalry behind them. Edith uses her influence to support her sister, marking a powerful moment of reconciliation and growth for both women.

A new era begins as Robert Crawley steps back from leadership, allowing Mary to take full control of the estate. Meanwhile, beloved staff members such as Carson and Mrs. Patmore retire, passing their responsibilities to the next generation.

The film closes with warmth and grace, offering happy endings for most characters and a deeply emotional farewell to the estate that has been home to so many unforgettable stories.

Zootopia 2

After their whirlwind first case, police officers Judy Hopps and Nick Wilde are now partners tackling the twists and turns of Zootopia together. However, their new partnership is tested almost immediately when a mysterious reptile, Gary De’Snake, arrives in the mammal metropolis and turns the city upside down.

To solve the confounding case, Judy and Nick must go undercover, a task that forces them to confront their own professional and personal issues in a series of partners’ counseling sessions with Dr. Fuzzby. As they navigate new and unexpected parts of town, like the semi-aquatic Marsh Market, they uncover a conspiracy tied to Zootopia’s hidden reptilian underclass.

The investigation pushes them to challenge their own biases and learn what it truly means to trust each other, all while keeping a city already recovering from division from falling into chaos once again.
